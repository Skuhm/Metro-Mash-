# Metro Mash

A team transit game for a day out in Los Angeles or Seattle. Two to six teams of two race the real train, bus and bike network, complete cards to buy stations, duel each other in trivia to steal them, and curse their rivals. There's also a two-player, one-phone mode.

**Play:** open the GitHub Pages link on your iPhone in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**. It opens full screen like an app.

Everything is one page (`index.html`). Team phones sync live through a free public relay, encrypted with the game code.
